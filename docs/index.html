2.6 mockup

The real 2.6 phone page, running against a stand-in for PowerPoint inside this file. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use the phone as you would in a talk, or try the steps on the right.

What the room sees

3.1Notes that follow the clicks

Write [click] in your PowerPoint notes where each animation step starts. The phone shows only what to say now, labelled with the step, and the next step’s first line under it. “Whole note” shows all of it.

3.2Text size, mid-talk

One setting from 90 to 200 %. The notes follow it fully, the rest of the screen up to 150 %; from 150 % the slide picture steps aside. On the phone it is Menu › Text size, a bar that leaves the notes in view.

3.3Turn the phone, or use a tablet

Each shape has its own layout: sideways puts the slide on the left, a tablet gets the slide and the next one side by side, and a tablet held sideways becomes a presenter view. After a turn, Back and Next ignore presses for a second and say so.

3.4Find a slide during questions

Slides › search finds a word in a title, on a slide or in the notes, or a slide number. With the keyboard open, the first tap only closes it. After a jump to a hidden backup slide, Back says “Back to slide N” and takes you there.

3.5Pass the slides to a co-presenter

Each phone has a name. From the Menu, pass the slides to another phone: the others follow along under a band, with a hatched edge, and their presses are refused. Holding Next for a moment takes the slides back.

Then try Next on Tomer’s phone, or hold it to take the slides back.

3.6Section budgets

With PowerPoint sections and one rehearsal, an amber notch on each edge of Next marks where the time should be when the section ends. More than 30 s past it, the line above the notes says “move on”; the only number stays on Next.

3.7When the clock starts

Setup asks first: when the slide show starts (talking on the title slide counts), with your first Next, or no clock at all, for a lesson. While the show runs and the clock waits, a band says so and offers “Start it now”. Pick one: the show starts again from slide 1.

3.8Zoom on the big screen (a trial)

Switched on in Setup › More options. Then + and − sit over the slide picture and zoom PowerPoint’s slide show; zoomed in, hold and drag the picture to move around. Fit, or the next slide, goes back to the whole slide. Watch the room’s screen above.

Things that happen in a real talk

4.1One set of design rules

The phone and the pairing page share these tokens by name (DESIGN.txt in the folder). Red means over time or trouble, amber means look here; nothing else uses them. Words on Next read at 7:1 or more in every state, checked by the tests.

4.2Less motion

Follows the phone’s own “reduce motion” setting; there is nothing to choose in Cuewand. With it on, slides, ripples and blinks become steady marks. Set Tomer’s phone either way, then press Next to compare.

4.3What a screen reader hears

Read live from Tomer’s phone as you use it. The time is spoken in minutes, so the name does not tick every second; a slide change is announced once.
