If the phone cannot open the page

University, hotel and conference Wi-Fi often stops devices from reaching each other. Two ways round it:

  1. Your phone’s hotspot. Turn the hotspot on, connect this computer to it, and scan again: the code here changes by itself. The slides do not use your mobile data.
  2. The internet link. Choose “Over the internet” above the code. The phone needs data; this computer needs the internet.

Windows firewall: when it asks about Python, tick Public as well as Private, then Allow. Hotel, venue and hotspot networks count as Public.

Keep the Cuewand window open while you present; close it to stop. Anyone who has a code can run your slides while Cuewand runs, until you make new codes. On the Same Wi-Fi code nothing is encrypted, so on a network you do not trust use your phone’s hotspot or the internet link.
